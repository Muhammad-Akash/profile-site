The short version

FIND THE ITCH.
SHIP THE FIX.

Most of what's here started as a personal annoyance.
Then it got out of hand, in a good way.

  1. 01

    Notice the annoyance.

    A store app that can't do the one thing it's for. A volume slider with five steps. A model too big for one machine.

  2. 02

    Build the smallest fix that works.

    No framework ceremony. One page, one binary, one APK — whatever gets it into your hands fastest.

  3. 03

    Keep it alive.

    Reconnects, drift, edge cases, and the second version nobody asked for but everybody wanted.
